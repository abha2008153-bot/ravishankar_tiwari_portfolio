01 / PROFILE

A professional
legal practice.

Advocate Ravi Shankar Tiwari is a practicing advocate based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, with professional practice before the High Court of Chhattisgarh at Bilaspur and the District & Sessions Courts.

He was enrolled as an advocate with the State Bar Council of Madhya Pradesh in 1999 and has built a long-standing legal practice spanning more than two decades.

His professional interests and work include civil, criminal and matrimonial matters, with a focus on legal consultation, case preparation and representation before the appropriate courts and forums.
